# As 4 estruturas de copy (todas com lastro)

## A. Roteiro-mestre (o do campeão de US$ 4.074 de gasto)
Curiosidade → três "você não precisa" → mecanismo (o rastro) → prova social → CTA.
> "There's one detail on Instagram that gives almost everyone away. The problem is, hardly anyone knows where to look. You don't need to guess. You don't need to argue. You don't need to grab their phone. Because when someone is trying to hide something, they almost always leave small traces without realizing it. That's why so many people discover messages, contacts and strange behavior even when the other person thinks they deleted everything. You just need to know where to look. The access is right below, while this tool is still available."
~95 palavras, 32 s. Troque "Instagram" por "WhatsApp" e as provas ("deleted messages, secret contacts, late-night chats") para mudar de oferta.

## B. Copy dos 10 elementos
Curiosidade · promessa com prazo · segmentação · problema · spoiler do mecanismo · autoridade · benefício · prova social · urgência · **dupla CTA** (ação + consequência).
> "There's one thing on WhatsApp that gives a cheater away every single time, and in sixty seconds you'll see it with your own eyes. If you're the one lying awake at two a.m. while their phone lights up face down, you already know something is wrong. You just can't prove it. Here's the thing: they can delete a chat, but the backup keeps a copy, and that is where the traces live. It's the same recovery method investigators use, and now it's one tap away. You don't touch their phone. You don't fight. You just type their number. Thousands of people did it this week… It won't stay online for long. Tap the link below and check the number now. If it's nothing, you sleep tonight. If it's something, you finally know."
~140 palavras, 43 s.

## C. Gancho de confissão (para a CAIXA escrita, não para a fala)
Primeira pessoa, presente, **reticências**, sem nomear o produto: *"I only checked because something felt off..."*, *"I found something I wasn't even looking for..."*. Os 4 criativos que mais gastaram usam isso; os 6 que não gastaram usam slogan em caixa alta.

## D. Hook de acusação direta (raiva)
Duas linhas na faixa, a primeira acusa e a segunda fecha a saída — e **a fala abre repetindo a mesma frase**:
> SHE'S CHEATING ON YOU / YOU JUST DON'T WANT TO SEE IT
> EVERYONE KNOWS BUT YOU / AND THEY'RE LAUGHING
> SHE CALLS YOU CRAZY / BECAUSE IT'S WORKING

Depois do soco: prova do cotidiano (celular virado, modo silencioso, banho com o telefone) → "você não está louco" (mata o gaslighting) → mecanismo → CTA sem tocar no aparelho dela. As 10 copys completas estão em `moldes/1-secret-revealed/copys.json`.
